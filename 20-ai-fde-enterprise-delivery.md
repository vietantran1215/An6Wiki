# AI FDE & Enterprise Delivery

## Role

An AI Forward Deployed Engineer works close to real customer workflows and converts ambiguous business problems into reliable AI systems.

The role combines:

- software engineering;
- architecture;
- LLM/RAG/agent engineering;
- enterprise integration;
- debugging;
- stakeholder communication;
- delivery under constraints.

## Engagement flow

```text
Business pain
 → workflow discovery
 → constraints
 → data/tool map
 → success metric
 → prototype
 → evaluation
 → security review
 → controlled rollout
 → production observation
 → iteration
```

## Enterprise constraints

Common constraints:

- privacy;
- compliance;
- legacy systems;
- fragmented data;
- permission boundaries;
- latency;
- cost;
- model reliability;
- human accountability;
- organizational ownership.

## Architecture rule

Start with the simplest architecture that satisfies the workflow.

Escalate to more complex agentic or multi-agent architecture only when justified by:

- distinct responsibilities;
- independent permissions;
- different tool sets;
- durable workflows;
- complex recovery;
- human approvals;
- cross-team ownership.

## Delivery artifacts

A strong engagement should leave behind:

- architecture;
- code;
- tests/evals;
- operating runbook;
- observability;
- security controls;
- documentation;
- handover path.
