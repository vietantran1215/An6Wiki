# ML, Computer Vision & Time-Series Foundations

This area appears less frequently than LLM/RAG topics, but it remains part of the broader applied-AI knowledge base.

## Classical ML mindset

Before selecting a model, define:

- target variable;
- features;
- train/validation/test split;
- baseline;
- evaluation metric;
- leakage risks;
- deployment constraints.

## Computer vision

Recurring practical topics include:

- image classification;
- object/content detection;
- managed vision APIs;
- custom labels;
- dataset quality;
- confidence thresholds;
- false positives/negatives.

## Time-series

Recurring practical topics include:

- anomaly detection;
- predictive maintenance;
- trend/seasonality;
- windowing;
- leakage across time;
- forecasting metrics.

## Production rule

Model quality must be evaluated together with data quality, latency, cost, monitoring, and business consequences.
