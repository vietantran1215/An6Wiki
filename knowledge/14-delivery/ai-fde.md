# AI Forward Deployed Engineering

## Role

An AI FDE converts ambiguous customer problems into working, supportable AI systems inside real enterprise constraints.

The role combines:

- software engineering;
- architecture;
- AI engineering;
- integration;
- debugging;
- stakeholder discovery;
- rollout;
- handover.

## Engagement lifecycle

```text
business pain
 → workflow discovery
 → constraint map
 → data/tool access
 → success metric
 → prototype
 → evaluation
 → security review
 → controlled rollout
 → production observation
 → iteration
```

## Discovery questions

- Who performs the workflow today?
- What decision/action is expensive?
- What evidence is required?
- What systems own the data?
- What permissions exist?
- What failure is unacceptable?
- What metric defines improvement?

## Enterprise constraints

- data privacy;
- legacy systems;
- fragmented identity;
- incomplete data;
- regulatory controls;
- latency;
- cost;
- human accountability.

## Pilot-to-production gap

A demo often ignores:

- access control;
- evaluation;
- monitoring;
- change management;
- recovery;
- ownership.

An FDE must close that gap.

## Deliverables

Leave behind:

- architecture;
- code;
- tests/evals;
- deployment;
- runbook;
- observability;
- security controls;
- handover documentation.

## Rule

The FDE's product is not only the AI feature. It is the customer capability to operate it reliably.
